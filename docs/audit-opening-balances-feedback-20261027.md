## Dated threshold policy

`usnVatPolicies` stores append-only versions scoped by stable `orgId: air-climat` and organization `АИР-КЛИМАТ`, with an effective date, exact ruble threshold, reason, author and creation timestamp. Existing defaults remain 60m (2025), 20m (2026), 15m (2027), 10m (2028); other years have no invented default. A custom version persists until the next version.

The existing management convention is preserved: previous-year net income is checked against the policy effective on January 1; current-year cumulative income is checked at dated cash events and policy boundaries, with strict greater-than comparison. Same-date refunds and receipts are aggregated, avoiding source-order dependence. Once triggered, the VAT start date remains for that year. A later higher policy cannot retroactively erase it. Invalid configuration explicitly makes the calculation incomplete.

The tax settings UI shows the selected-date policy, previous/current income, trigger date and version history. It requires authenticated admin/accountant, reason and valid amount/date, and checks the affected period range before append. A later midyear policy still leaves the effect through year end, so the preflight extends through the January following that later policy's year. Changes during async checking preserve the user's form and reject the mutation. Root integration owns the corresponding server guard and sync author controls.
